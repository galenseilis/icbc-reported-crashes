# Tutorials

Tutorials are learning-oriented. They take you through a complete, working
sequence from a known starting point and explain what you are doing along the
way. They are not reference material and do not try to cover every option.

## Available tutorials

### [Build and explore your first export](first-export.md)

Start with a fresh clone, run the pipeline, verify the outputs, and query the
data with pandas. This is the best place to begin if you have not used the
project before.

**Time:** about 10–20 minutes, depending on download and disk speed.

**You will need:** git, uv, network access, and about 700 MB of free disk space.

## After the tutorial

Once you have a working export, move on to the
[how-to guides](../how-to-guides/index.md) for specific tasks, or keep the
[reference](../reference/index.md) open while you work. If you want to know why
the data looks the way it does, read
[Data limitations](../explanation/data-limitations.md).
